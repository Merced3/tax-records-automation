# Questions for Tax Professional

## Overall

- Which years definitely require filing based on the IRS transcripts and records collected?
- Which years look most likely to result in tax owed vs refund vs unknown?
- Are any refunds at risk of expiring due to late filing?

## Mileage / vehicle deductions

- For each year, should I use standard mileage or actual expenses?
- How much mileage can be reasonably reconstructed for years with weak logs?
- What supporting documents are strongest if mileage logs are incomplete?

## 2023 special issue

- How should income earned through a friend’s DoorDash/Uber account in 2023 be handled?
- What documentation would help address that issue cleanly?

## Uber / gig income

- How should Uber 1099-K gross receipts be reconciled against actual net earnings and deductible platform expenses?
- What is the best way to document business mileage for Uber years?

## Brokerage / investing

- Are Robinhood, Tradier, and IBKR records sufficient as currently collected?
- Is anything else needed to report options trades, dividends, and investment activity correctly?

## 2025 software job

- How should the 2025 software job be handled if I am still gathering pay stubs or year-end forms?
- What exact documents do you want from that job?
